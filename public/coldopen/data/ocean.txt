The Deep Ocean Is the Last Unmapped Frontier
More than 80 percent of the ocean has never been mapped or explored by humans.
Only about 25 percent of the seafloor has been surveyed at high resolution.
The hadal zone below 6,000 meters hosts life in every trench sampled so far.
A single liter of deep seawater can hold more than 50,000 microbes.
Deep-sea sediments store more carbon than all of the worlds soils combined.
Less than 0.0001 percent of the deep ocean has ever been visited by a human being.
